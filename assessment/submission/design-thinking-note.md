# Submission Note

The central idea is simple: Crystal’s work connects spaces, ideas, and people. The page begins with her practice, then introduces selected projects before moving to the wider ecosystem, so visitors understand both the work itself and how the initiatives relate. This hierarchy creates an editorial rhythm without turning seven distinct ventures into interchangeable cards.

The visual direction uses warm ivory, charcoal, clay, and olive with Cormorant Garamond and Manrope. Supplied portraits and project photography carry the story, while ample spacing and clear reading paths give the page a quiet, premium editorial tone. The site uses Tailwind CSS 4, semantic sections, keyboard-visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and a compact Escape-close mobile menu. The App Router page remains statically prerendered for performance, and direct enquiry details or a verified live domain were not supplied, so the build avoids invented links and leaves canonical or social metadata unset until deployment details are known. This keeps the reading experience calm and clear while helping the visitor understand how architecture, advocacy, and community fit together.
